# Welcome to Lunix! 👋

**Leiden's first AI community**  

We exist to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world application. As AI reshapes every industry from Law and Medicine to Computer Science and Ethics, LUNIX provides the central ecosystem where Leiden’s most ambitious students connect, build, and lead.

**Our Mission**

To empower the next generation of innovators by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration. We bring together developers, researchers, and future policy-makers to explore the frontier of AI.
